# Home-made Oat Milk

Home-made oat milk is much cheaper than store-bought and pretty much tastes the
same. The main difficulty is to avoid making it slimy.

## Ingredients

- Oat meal
- Water
- Salt

## Recipe

Combine 6 parts water to 1 part oat, blend for 30 seconds (no more, no less).

Then strain it with a cheese cloth without compressing the pulp.

Salt to your preference.
