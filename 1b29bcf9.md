# Fragrances

Fragrances are the thing that gives your candle personality. It is central to
giving a positive experience.

There are two options when choosing ingredients:
- Essential oils: For a more natural scent
- Fragrance oils: Fragrance oils can mimic almost any scent imaginable

In both cases, it's easy to use too much. Use lightly.

## Warning for essential oils

Since the goal of a BDSM candle is to be used on the skin, extra care should be
taken when choosing essential oils, as some of them can irritate the skin if not
diluted in a carrier oil.
