# Controlling the temperature of the wax

While it is not needed for regular candle-making, being able to reasonably
predict the temperature of the candle's wax is essential for BDSM wax-play.

For BDSM, use only coconut, soy or paraffin wax, as almost anything above that
point will be dangerous.

Mixing waxes makes it possible to finely adjust the wax melting point.
