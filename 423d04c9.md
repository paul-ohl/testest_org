# Candle-making process

Below are detailed the steps to make candles from the ingredients listed in
[Candle-making tools](9f080a79).

## Safety precautions

Melted and especially over-heated candle wax is dangerous, handle with
precaution and don't leave unattended.

Melting candle wax can release toxic fumes, execute the following steps in a
well-ventilated area.

## Preparation

The [Mold or container](646a6294) should be cleaned beforehand.
- If using a mold, add a small oil coating to help with release.
- If using a glass or ceramic container, heat it to limit thermal shock when
  pouring the hot wax.

Weigh all the quantities beforehand to simplify the process once the wax-melting
begins.

[Prepare the wicks](9b6285a9).

## Melting the wax

The best easy method for wax melting is the "bain marie" technique.

A container with the wax in it is put in a pan with boiling water so that the
wax melts without mixing with the water.

Other methods exist but require expensive equipment.

## Adding colors

Liquid dyes should be added *once the wax is melted*, dye chips should be melted
*alongside* the wax.

Colors can be layered to create beautiful visual effects.

## Adding fragrance

Add scenteds oils to the *melted* wax and stir gently.

## Pouring

Pour slowly to avoid creating bubbles and displacing the wick.

## Cooling and demolding

Candle wax typically takes 24 to 48 hours to properly set. Wait for the wax to
be completely cooled to demold the candle.

## Finishing touches

Trim the wick so that it can burn without being drowned in wax, but not too long
that it starts producing soot when it burns or makes too big of a flame.
