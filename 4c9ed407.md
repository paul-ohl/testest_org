# Candle making

My goal making candles is to create nice, beautiful candles that will either
smell nice or have a good temperature for BDSM wax-play (or both).

The different elements composing a candle are the jar or mold, the wick and the
wax itself. Additives are used to improve certain properties of the wax
(texture, scent, etc). Finally, fragrances are used to give a specific scent to
the candle beyond the original scent of the wax.
