# Why SUVs are so popular in the USA

SUVs are categorized as "light trucks" in america (through immense lobbying),
which allows them to skip a lot of regulation on:
* Gas mileage
* Luxury vehicle tax
* [Safety regulations](d494b8d4)

Also, since SUVs are bigger vehicles, car companies can generally ramp up the
price massively, creating far bigger margins than other vehicle types.

## How this concerns Europe

Although Europe has much stricter regulations concerning vehicle safety, SUVs
are still gaining in popularity through advertising and influence from the USA.

The arrival of SUVs can be fought through even stricter regulations, and tolls
for bigger vehicles.
