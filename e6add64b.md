# Outer Wilds

I'm trying to solve the mystery happening in the world of Outer Wilds.

Here are my notes.

## The time-loop

The game starts 21 minutes before the sun explodes the whole solar system.
There is a time loop. You are taken back at the start of the loop everytime the
sun explodes, even if you are outside the blast radius.
