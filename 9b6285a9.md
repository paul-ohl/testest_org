# Preparing candle wicks

Here are steps to prepare the wick before pouring wax:

- Dip the wick in wax to coat it (optional); This can improve the burn and help
  it stand straight.
- Straighten the wick
- Anchor it firmly in place: The wick shouldn't move when pouring the wax
