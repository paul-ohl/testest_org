# The Blockchain

The blockchain garantees [Integrity](952ca7af) within distributed, peer-to-peer
systems.

The main issue with peer-to-peer being to garantee integrity, the blockchain has
the opportunity to revolutionize a bunch of industries by causing
*disintermediation* (meaning removing middle-men from transactions).
