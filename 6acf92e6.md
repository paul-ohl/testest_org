# Additives

Candle additives are used to improve certain qualities of the original wax in
order to get a great candle.

## Types of additives

Additives can be combined to get a compounding effect, but they can generally
react to candle wax in unexpected ways.

Always test a new wax/additive combo by experimenting with low quantities before
making a big batch.

### Stability

- Stearic Acid: Basic hardener, no additional properties
- Vybar 103: Hardens and improves the resulting look of the candle, while
  improving fragrance retention
- Microcrystalline wax: Adds stability and hardness, mostly useful for container
  candles

### Visual

- Liquid dye: Gives a color to the wax; **Warning**: Use specialized candle dye
- Dye chips: Concentrated color, is meant to be melted along with the candle
  wax, more advanced
- UV Stabilizer: Improves color retention when exposing candles to the sun
- Mottling oil: Adds beautiful patterns to the candle

### Scent

- D-Limonene: Improves "fragrance throw", to improve the way the scent is
  dispersed

### Burn

- Soy Lecithin: Improves candle burn and increases burn duration.
